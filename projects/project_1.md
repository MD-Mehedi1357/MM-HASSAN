## 📌 Project Overview
This project is a structured digital archive and searchable index focusing on the **Constitutional Law of Bangladesh** and landmark judicial decisions of the Supreme Court of Bangladesh (Appellate Division and High Court Division). It is designed to help law students, legal researchers, and practitioners quickly reference key constitutional doctrines, fundamental rights interpretations, and historic case precedents.

## 🎯 Key Objectives
- **Centralized Case Indexing:** Organize landmark constitutional cases (e.g., *Dr. Mohiuddin Farooque v. Bangladesh*, *Secretary, Ministry of Finance v. Masdar Hossain*) by article and legal doctrine.
- **Doctrine Categorization:** Highlight emerging principles in Bangladeshi public law, including judicial review, basic structure doctrine, legitimate expectation, proportionality, and public trust.
- **Accessible Legal Research:** Bridge the gap between traditional legal scholarship and modern open-access digital repositories using clean Markdown documentation and structured metadata.

## 🛠️ Tools & Methodology
- **Research & Documentation:** Markdown, PDF Archiving, Legal Citation Indexing (DLR, BLD, BLC, MLR)
- **Version Control:** Git & GitHub
- **Reference Materials:** *Constitutional Law of Bangladesh* by Mahmudul Islam, Supreme Court Law Reports, and open-access legal archives.
