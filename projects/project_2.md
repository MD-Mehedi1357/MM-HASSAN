## 📌 Project Overview
The **Academic & Legal Society Event Hub** is a portfolio project designed to streamline the planning, outreach, and resource management for university law society seminars, guest lectures, moot court competitions, and student workshops at Khulna University. It provides standardized templates, event workflows, and an archive of academic sessions.

## 🎯 Key Features
- **Speaker & Faculty Outreach System:** Standardized communication workflows and invitation templates for engaging professors, legal scholars, and guest panelists.
- **Seminar & Workshop Archive:** A structured catalog of past and upcoming academic events, panel discussions, and student participation records.
- **Moot Court & Debate Resources:** Curated guidelines, memorial formatting templates, and oral advocacy checklists for law students.
- **Branding & Media Assets:** Centralized repository for official university and club visual assets, posters, and event schedules.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
- **Frontend / Documentation:** HTML5, CSS3, Markdown (GitHub Pages ready)
- **Project Management:** GitHub Projects, Issues, and Milestones
- **Design & Assets:** Vector Graphics (`.eps` / `.svg`) for institutional branding and event banners

## 📈 Impact & Use Case
By maintaining a version-controlled repository of club operations and academic resources, student representatives can smoothly transition leadership roles, maintain professional communication standards with faculty members, and preserve institutional memory across batches.
